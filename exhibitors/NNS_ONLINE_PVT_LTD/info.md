## Company Profile
NNS MEDIA GROUP BEGAN ITS OPERATIONS IN THE YEAR 1950 AS A NEWS AGENCY, NATIONAL NEWS SERVICE (NNS). TODAY, NNS PROVIDES COMMODITY NEWS TO ALMOST ALL THE LEADING NATIONAL DAILY NEWSPAPERS AND GLOBAL NEWS AGENCIES LIKE BLOOMBERG.
‘BUSINESS STAR’, A PUBLICATION OF ‘NNS MEDIA GROUP’, IS INDIA’S LEADING ENGLISH MONTHLY MAGAZINE EXCLUSIVELY DEVOTED TO AGRI-BUSINESS AND FOOD PROCESSING SECTOR. LAUNCHED IN THE YEAR 1989, THE MAGAZINE HAS DEDICATED READERSHIP OF PRE-PAID SUBSCRIBERS. NNS MEDIA GROUP ALSO OWNS INDIA’S LEADING BUSINESS HINDI DAILY NEWSPAPER, ‘VYAPAR KESARI’, DEVOTED TO COMMODITY NEWS.

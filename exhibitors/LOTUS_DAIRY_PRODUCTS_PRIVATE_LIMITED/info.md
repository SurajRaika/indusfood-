## Company Profile
FOUNDED IN 1968 IN RAJASTHAN, LOTUS DAIRY IS A FAMILY-OWNED FMCG COMPANY COMMITTED TO DELIVERING HIGH-QUALITY DAIRY AND FOOD PRODUCTS. FROM A MODEST BEGINNING IN MILK PROCUREMENT, THE COMPANY HAS GROWN INTO A TRUSTED BRAND WITH A DIVERSE PORTFOLIO INCLUDING MILK, GHEE, BEVERAGES, SWEETS, AND VALUE-ADDED DAIRY PRODUCTS. LOTUS WORKS CLOSELY WITH OVER 35,000 FARMERS ACROSS 1,650+ VILLAGES, ENSURING QUALITY FROM FARM TO CONSUMER. WITH MODERN MANUFACTURING FACILITIES AND A STRONG FOCUS ON INNOVATION, LOTUS CONTINUES TO SERVE CUSTOMERS ACROSS INDIA AND INTERNATIONAL MARKETS.
